# Characters — *The Strays*

Character canon, sourced from the Chapter 1 rough draft ("The Move") and the
series premise. Read alongside `Canon.md` (story engine) and `style-guide.md`
(first-person Gabriel). Details marked _(seed)_ are author notes not yet dramatized
— treat as intended, not yet hard canon.

---

## Gabriel — narrator / protagonist

The "I" of the entire series. Everything reaches the reader through him.

- **Who he is:** A failed novelist. Once a "promising writer" the *Times* tagged
  with "unlimited potential"; a string of failed projects later, his one proven
  skill is "proving my early supporters wrong." Hasn't worked much since his last
  unread novel.
- **Voice & temperament:** Private, self-deprecating, dryly funny, anxious. An
  interior man who narrates to keep himself calm — and it doesn't work. Prone to
  health-anxiety spirals (spent a day convinced an online doctor had given him a
  fatal brain tumor).
- **Relationship to the ordinary:** Feels like a failure and a fraud, sharpened
  by being surrounded by accomplished people (his surgeon wife, her surgeon
  colleagues). The move is meant to be his fresh start too, not just Rebecca's.
- **Habits / vulnerabilities:** Took "irresponsibly strong" sleeping medication;
  after the overlook incident he poured the pills down the drain and swore off
  them (now quitting cold turkey, which his doctor warned was "inadvisable" — an
  ongoing source of insomnia and instability). Drinks beer, out of shape.
- **The wound the engine turns:** He cannot tell whether what he experiences is
  real or his mind breaking, and he **hides it from Rebecca** — he lied to her
  about the night walk. Honesty with her is his stated life goal, so each lie
  costs him. His silence is the crack the horror widens.
- **Engine notes:** He is the unreliable narrator. He can misperceive, lose time,
  and rationalize. Keep him intelligent enough to doubt himself — that doubt is
  scarier than naivety.

## Rebecca — Gabriel's wife

The anchor to the real; the person he's protecting and lying to.

- **Who she is:** A surgeon; just took her **dream job overseeing a hospital's
  surgery center** — the reason for the move. Excellent at what she does.
- **Temperament:** Warm, optimistic, playful (rock-paper-scissors for chores;
  smirks when she's caught him out). Relaxed and happy in the new house and job —
  a normalcy Gabriel "desperately needed." Sharp: she immediately clocked that he
  hid his disappointment at the town, and half-remembers his night walk ("Did you
  go for a walk last night or did I dream that?").
- **Story function:** She is the tether to normal life and the measure of what
  Gabriel stands to lose. Her happiness is exactly why he won't tell her the
  truth. Her trust is a resource the story can spend. **Not a damsel, not a naive
  skeptic** — she has her own interiority (seen only through Gabriel).
- **Engine notes:** Keep her perceptive. She is well-positioned to be the one who
  wonders whether Gabriel is unwell — a lever the ambiguity engine can pull.

## Lela — the dog

- **Who she is:** Gabriel and Rebecca's dog; travelled the cross-country move
  well. Constant companion on the trails.
- **Story function:** The **guardian / early-warning system.** At the overlook she
  barked uncontrollably and physically dragged Gabriel back from the edge as he
  moved to jump — nearly breaking his arm on the leash. She was "unfazed" by the
  fog and light, which is itself ambiguous: proof he imagined it, or proof the
  thing wasn't for her.
- **Engine notes:** Animals sensing (or not sensing) the supernatural is a clean
  ambiguity tool. Use Lela's reactions deliberately — what she does and doesn't
  react to is evidence the reader will weigh.

## Redding locals

## Martha — real estate agent

- **Who she is:** Their agent; handed over the house keys. Late forties. "One of
  those kinds of people that exist in every town" — fundamentally nice, but a
  **chronic oversharer** who "had yet to meet a secret worth keeping." Overshared
  about a new skin disorder.
- **Story function:** First local Gabriel meets; embodies small-town over-
  familiarity that unsettles a private man. Her compulsive openness makes her a
  potential source of town history/lore (delivered as gossip, never as a clean
  exposition dump — per Canon). Gatekeeper to the house.

## Hospital coworkers (Rebecca's colleagues)

Met at a work dinner at the **Crabcake Factory** by the shore.

## Paul

- **Who he is:** One of the best **surgeons/neurosurgeons in the state.** Portly,
  rapidly receding red hair, ill-fitting polo, enviably confident. Friendly.
- **The dirt (via Sarah):** A "serial monogamist" who is also a serial cheater —
  unafraid of marriage but perpetually broke from the fallout of his infidelities
  and divorces. Left the dinner early when his pager went off.
- **Story function:** Makes Gabriel feel like a fraud ("a complete fraud for ever
  calling myself a writer"). A neurosurgeon is a loaded presence in a story about a
  man fearing for his own mind — potential future resource or threat.

## Sarah

- **Who she is:** Rebecca's coworker. Heavy drinker (booze breath; intoxication
  "rising at an unsustainable rate"), the office's **chronic gossip and
  oversharer.** On a new diet; openly admits she drinks too much.
- **Story function:** The exposition-via-gossip valve — she's how Gabriel (and the
  reader) learns the social map of the hospital. Called Rebecca "a saint." Her
  unreliability as a narrator-within-the-narrative mirrors Gabriel's own.

## Craig — _(not yet met)_

- **Who he is:** A coworker Gabriel hasn't met. Per Sarah, "one of the good ones"
  whom Gabriel would "love-love-love."
- **TRUE NATURE _(author-only; reveal later)_:** Craig **is Gabriel's
  doppelgänger.** Confirmed canon, but **do not reveal or hint on the page yet** —
  he's to be introduced and explored later. Until then he's only a name others
  mention warmly; the over-selling ("one of the good ones," "love-love-love him")
  is deliberate setup. When he finally appears, the not-quite-right familiarity
  pays it off. See continuity ledger and the Forbidden list in `Canon.md`.

## Casting the ambiguity (quick reference for the engine)

| Character | Real-world read | Supernatural read |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Gabriel | Stressed, insomniac ex-writer, pill withdrawal | A mind being claimed by the town |
| Rebecca | Happy, busy surgeon who married a struggling man | The person the town will use against him |
| Lela | A dog reacting to a sleepwalking owner | An animal that sees what Gabriel can't prove |
| The water/overlook light | Sedative hallucination | Something in the water that beckons |

Every character should be playable in **both** columns. That is the job.
