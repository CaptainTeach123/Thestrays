# The Strays — Wattpad Description

## Blurb (main)

*Moving was supposed to be their fresh start.*

Gabriel and his wife Rebecca leave everything behind for Redding, a fading town on the Maine coast where she's landed her dream job and he's hoping to finally finish the novel that's been quietly killing him. The house is perfect. The water is close. For the first time in years, things are looking up.

Then, on their very first night in town, something in the water calls Gabriel to the edge of a cliff and very nearly convinces him to step off.

He tells himself it was the pills. He tells himself it was the exhaustion, the stress, the fog he's fairly sure he imagined. He tells himself a lot of things. But the fog keeps coming back when he's stone sober, the town keeps looking wrong in ways no one else seems to notice, and the line between what's real and what's inside his head gets thinner every sleepless night.

Something claimed Redding long before Gabriel arrived. Now it's reaching for him too, offering the one thing he's never had: quiet. To save himself, and the wife he can't bring himself to tell the truth, he'll have to uncover what's haunting this town before it finishes hollowing him out.

Assuming anything is haunting it at all.

*Can Gabriel find the truth behind Redding and hold on to his sanity? Or will the unknown take everything he loves, one lie at a time?*

Gabriel moved to Redding for a fresh start. On his first night, something in the fog called him to the edge of a cliff and almost won.

Now he's the only one who sees the town for what it is, or the only one losing his mind. In a place this quiet, the difference could cost him his wife, his sanity, and everything he swore he'd never lose.

*Some towns don't take strangers in. They take them apart.*

## Option B — Literary / atmospheric

The town of Redding sits quiet on the Maine coast, half-empty, salt-worn, the kind of place people pass through and rarely stay. Gabriel and Rebecca came to stay.

She has her dream job and a house by the water. He has a failed novel, a bottle of sleeping pills he's just poured down the drain, and, since the first night, a memory he can't square with the world: a fog that never registered on any forecast, a light where no light should be, and the sea reaching for him like it knew his name.

He's decided it was nothing. He decides that most mornings now.

But something has lived in this town a long time, and it has found in Gabriel exactly what it likes best, a man already learning not to trust himself.

*A horror story about the quiet that comes right before you go under.*

## Option C — In Gabriel's voice (matches the book)

I hadn't slept in a month, I'd lied to my wife twice, and I was fairly sure the fog outside wasn't real. That last one was the problem.

We moved to Redding for a fresh start. Rebecca got her dream job; I got a house by the water and a first night I still can't explain. Now the town keeps looking wrong in ways only I can see, and there's a very good liar living behind my eyes, and I can't tell anymore which of us is doing the talking.

I'd like to tell you it gets better.

*Can I find out what's wrong with this town before I lose the part of me that could?*

## Option D — Hook / question-forward

What if the most peaceful you've ever felt was something else, wearing peace like bait?

Gabriel doesn't believe in ghosts. He believes in stress, bad sleep, and the pills he shouldn't have taken. So when the water outside his new home nearly walked him off a cliff, he had a hundred reasons why.

He's running out of reasons.

The town of Redding is beautiful, quiet, and wrong, and Gabriel is the only one who feels it, which means either something is hunting him, or nothing is, and he's already gone. He isn't sure which scares him more.

*Welcome to Redding. Try to remember it's not your friend.*
